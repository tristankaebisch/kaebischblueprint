[ Common Inquiries ]

Frequently Asked Questions

How do weekly check-ins work?

Every week on your designated check-in morning, you submit your rolling scale averages, biofeedback ratings (sleep, stress, digestion), and training footage. You receive a detailed breakdown covering technique feedback and plan adjustments within 24 hours.

Do I need access to a specific gym or equipment?

Access to a well-equipped commercial gym (barbells, dumbbells, cable stacks, and stable compound machines like hack squats or leg presses) is recommended. However, plans are tailored around whatever equipment you have available.

What is the minimum commitment period?

Coaching requires a minimum 12-week commitment. Significant physiological adaptations—whether building dense muscle tissue or shedding fat while retaining lean mass—require consistent adherence over time.

Are meal plans rigid or flexible?

We utilize precision macronutrient frameworks built around staple whole foods. You receive guidance on high-adherence staples, meal timing, and substitutions rather than a monotonous, unsustainable rigid diet.
