[ Questions ]

Frequently Asked Questions

How do weekly check-ins work?

Every Sunday morning, you log your weight average, sleep, hunger levels, and training videos into your sheet. I review everything and send back a detailed feedback video and plan updates within 24 hours.

What kind of gym access do I need?

A standard gym with barbells, dumbbells, cables, and a few good machines (like a leg press or hack squat) is ideal. If your setup is limited, I build the routine around whatever you have access to.

What is the minimum commitment?

Coaching requires a minimum 12-week commitment. Real muscle growth and safe, lean fat loss take time. Twelve weeks gives us enough runway to build serious momentum.

Do you give rigid meal plans?

No boring chicken-and-rice only diets. I give you daily calorie and macronutrient targets along with grocery guides and meal timing advice so your diet actually fits your life.
